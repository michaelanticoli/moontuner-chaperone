Phase I: The Void DURATION: 15 MIN

Submerge the Tourmaline in the vessel. Breath must be held at the bottom of the exhale for four counts. This mimics the Scorpio 'Death' state.

Phase II: The Embodiment DURATION: 40 MIN

Wrap the stone in heavy linen. Press it against the base of the spine. Visualize the fluid energy of the psyche turning into solid, structural mineral.

Phase III: The Anchoring DURATION: INDEFINITE

Place the wrapped stone at the lowest point of your dwelling. It now acts as a tether for the 'Rooted Power' archetype.
