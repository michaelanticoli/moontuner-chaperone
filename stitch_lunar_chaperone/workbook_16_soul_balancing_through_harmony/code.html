The Somatic Protocol

The shift from the nebulous feet of Pisces to the symmetrical kidneys of Libra marks the move from ethereal dissolution to relational equilibrium.

1. The Descent Locate the space between the first and second metatarsal. Breathe into the "Water" element of the sole, releasing collective grief into the earth.

2. The Ascent Draw the energy through the inner calf to the lower back. Visualize the kidneys as twin scales of a golden balance.

3. The Equilibrium Inhale "Air"—the element of Libra—into the dorsal space, expanding the rib cage laterally like wings.

Ritual Script: The Mirror

Perform during the twilight hour of the 14th day

Light two candles of equal height. Place a bowl of water between them. Breathe until the surface of the water becomes a still mirror. Look not at your reflection, but at the light reflected within the water.

"What is heavy in me, let it be lifted. What is hollow in me, let it be filled. Between the two, I find the Center."
