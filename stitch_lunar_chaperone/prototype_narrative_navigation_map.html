# Prototype Narrative: The Sequential Journey of the Sacred Ledger This document outlines the intuitive and consecutive navigation flow for the "Lunar Chaperone" prototype. The core principle is a linear, unbroken chain of 26 workbooks that guide the user through the lunar year. ## 1. Entry Point: The Threshold * **The Great Library:** The evocative landing page. * **Action:** User enters the sanctum to access the master directory. ## 2. Navigation Hub: The Master Index * **The Canonical Archive: Master Index:** A unified, linear list of all 26 workbooks. * **The Logic:** No "Series" or "Arc" breaks. A single, continuous scroll showing the alternation between New Moon → Full Moon and Full Moon → New Moon. ## 3. The Sequential Loop (Workbooks 01–24) Each workbook represents a 14-day transition. Navigation is strictly "Next/Previous" based on lunation sequence: 1. **Workbook 01:** New Moon (Aries) → Full Moon (Scorpio) 2. **Workbook 02:** Full Moon (Scorpio) → New Moon (Taurus) 3. **Workbook 03:** New Moon (Taurus) → Full Moon (Sagittarius) 4. **Workbook 04:** Full Moon (Sagittarius) → New Moon (Gemini) 5. **Workbook 05:** New Moon (Gemini) → Full Moon (Capricorn) 6. **Workbook 06:** Full Moon (Capricorn) → New Moon (Cancer) 7. **Workbook 07:** New Moon (Cancer) → Full Moon (Aquarius) 8. **Workbook 08:** Full Moon (Aquarius) → New Moon (Leo) 9. **Workbook 09:** New Moon (Leo) → Full Moon (Pisces) 10. **Workbook 10:** Full Moon (Pisces) → New Moon (Virgo) 11. **Workbook 11:** New Moon (Virgo) → Full Moon (Aries) 12. **Workbook 12:** Full Moon (Aries) → New Moon (Scorpio) *(Sequence continues through 24 following this logic of alternating lunation types)* ## 4. Internal Workbook Flow Within each workbook, the user moves through a standardized sequence: * **Page 1: Context & Framing** (Lunar Awakening & Attunements) * **Page 2: Energy Mapping & Transition** (Somatic Inquiry) * **Page 3: Practices & Protocols** (Domain-specific tasks) * **Page 4: 14-Day Integration Plan** (The daily practice cycle) * **Page 5: Ritual Scripts & Commitments** (Sacred Vows) * **Page 6: Research & Seminar Prompts** (Academic synthesis) * **Final Action:** "Seal Entry & Advance to Next Workbook" ## 5. Threshold Crossings: The Eclipse Portals * **Workbook 25 & 26:** These appear in the sequence when the celestial geometry dictates (e.g., during the Virgo-Aries and Pisces-Virgo portals), offering intensified protocols for non-linear practitioners. ## 6. Global Navigation Strategy * **Top Nav:** Always provides a link back to "The Archive" (Master Index) and "The Library" (Entry Point). * **Bottom Bar:** Contextual actions based on current workbook progress. * **Sequential Footer:** Every workbook concludes with a clear call-to-action for the *next* lunation in the sequence, ensuring a never-ending cycle of guidance.
