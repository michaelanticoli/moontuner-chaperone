Lumen Primus

Day 1: The Light Reveals

Lunar Cycle Observation

Today's Inquiry

Under the zenith of the Full Moon, that which was hidden in the periphery is brought into the center.

Consider the architecture of your current commitments. When the light is most intense, the cracks in the foundation become visible. Reflect on the responsibilities you carry—are they vessels for your purpose, or merely weight you've forgotten to put down? The illumination today is clinical and unyielding; it demands an audit of your internal structures.

Ink on Vellum

Somatic Practice

01 Find a seated position on a hard surface. Feel the sit-bones anchor into the earth.

02 Lengthen the spine upward, imagining each vertebra as a bead of light being pulled gently by a thread from the crown of the head.

03 Breathe into the back body. Allow the ribs to expand laterally like an opening fan. Hold this tall architecture for seven breath cycles.
