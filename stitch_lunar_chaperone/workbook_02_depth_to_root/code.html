01. Lunar Context

Observed Path: Scorpio FM → Taurus NM

The Descents

The journey begins in the Scorpio Full Moon—a peak of emotional intensity and psychological transparency. This is the "Depth," where we unearth the hidden narratives of the psyche. As the moon wanes, we strip back the non-essential, moving toward the dark moon's silence.

The arc concludes at the Taurus New Moon, where we plant these refined insights into the physical realm. "Rooting" is the act of making the psychological tangible.

02. Somatic Attunements

Phase A

Visceral Release

Focus on the pelvic bowl and deep psoas. Scorpio demands the release of stored tension through slow, rhythmic fluid movement.

Phase B

The Neutral Void

Breathwork focused on the pause between exhales. Finding the stillness in the transition from water to soil.

Phase C

Sensory Grounding

Taurus arrival. Focus on skin contact, texture, and the weight of the body against the earth. Establishing somatic safety.
