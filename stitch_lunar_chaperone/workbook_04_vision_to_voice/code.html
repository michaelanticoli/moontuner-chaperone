1. Lunar Context

The Sagittarius Full Moon illuminates the macro-lens of our existence. It is the wide-angle view, the broad strokes of wisdom, and the unrefined fire of truth. As the cycle wanes toward the Gemini New Moon, the task is one of refinement.

We are moving from the "Why" to the "How." The expansive vision of the archer must now pass through the precise needle-eye of the twins, translating celestial insight into terrestrial dialogue.

"What part of your inner truth is lost in the translation to language?"
